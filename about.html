ABOUT CHINESE INN

A restaurant built around good food

Chinese Inn Restaurant is a family-run concept in Mbale focused on freshly prepared Chinese-inspired meals. The menu highlights bold Szechuan flavours while keeping the dining experience comfortable and approachable.

Whether you are meeting friends, enjoying a family meal or collecting takeaway, our goal is simple: serve satisfying food with warm hospitality.
